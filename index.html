📖 Understanding Your Dashboard
🚗 Speed and RPM: Shows how fast you are moving. Lower RPM means better fuel efficiency.
⛽ Fuel Economy (ICE) or Range (EV): Higher number means more kilometres per unit of energy.
🌡️ Temperature: For ICE: 85 to 95°C is normal. For EV Battery: 20 to 45°C is normal.
💰 Running Cost: ICE uses ₹105 per litre for Petrol or ₹90 per litre for Diesel. EV uses ₹8 per kWh for electricity.
🌍 BS-VI Standards (ICE): Petrol limit is 150 g/km. Diesel limit is 140 g/km. EV produces zero tailpipe emissions.
